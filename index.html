    About AquaConnect

    AquaConnect is a smart campus water-management website. It helps students find working drinking water, dispose of plastic bottles responsibly, and report problems that college management can fix quickly.

    Features

    FeatureDescription
    Water Point LocatorFind nearby drinking water facilities.
    QR Code ReportingScan a QR code on a cooler to report problems.
    Live StatusWorking, Not Working or Under Maintenance.
    Bottle Recycling LocatorFind the nearest plastic bottle collection bin.
    Water Quality FeedbackSubmit feedback about cleanliness.
    Complaint TrackingPending, In Progress and Resolved.
    Smart NotificationsNotified when your reported problem is resolved.
    Water Saving TipsSuggestions for avoiding water wastage.
    Admin DashboardCollege management can manage complaints.
    Rating SystemStudents rate water facilities.
    Crowd-Based UpdatesStudents confirm whether a point is working.
    Smart Route SuggestionsNearest available point from your block.
    Maintenance PriorityUrgent issues get higher priority.
    Eco PointsPoints for responsible bottle recycling.
    Availability HeatmapSee where working water points are scarce.
    Digital Complaint IDUnique ID for every complaint.
